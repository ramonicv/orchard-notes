# Third-party notices

## icloud-md

Parts of this app are ported from [icloud-md](https://github.com/coddingtonbear/icloud-md): the topotext CRDT edit engine (`notes/doc/NoteEditing.kt`, `TextDiff.kt`), the note document model (`NoteDocument.kt`), the formatting reconciler (`FormatReconcile.kt`), and the CloudKit record field sets (`notes/NoteFields.kt`). The real captured note payloads under `app/src/test/resources/fixtures` and `app/src/debug/assets/demo` also come from icloud-md.
